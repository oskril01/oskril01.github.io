Welcome

I am a PhD student at the University of Chicago’s Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering, advised by Samantha Riesenfeld and Fred Chong. My research focuses on combining quantum computing and classical machine learning to develop algorithms for solving problems in biology.

Before coming to UChicago, I studied chemical engineering, mathematics, and chemical physics as an undergraduate at Tufts University, where I later earned a master’s degree in mathematics with a focus on probability theory and statistical learning.

Outside of academia, I am an avid reader and artist. I enjoy drawing, painting, traveling, and visiting museums, as well as skiing, running, hiking, and doing flips.
